# 📊 Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 🧾 Project Overview
This is a beginner-friendly Power BI dashboard that visualizes key sales insights using SQL-aggregated tables. It focuses on trends, product performance, category breakdowns, and regional comparisons. The project highlights foundational skills in visualization, and storytelling for business reporting.

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI**
- **SQL** (used for creating summary/aggregated tables)

---

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights

- 📅 **Sales Trend Over Time**  
  Line chart visualizing monthly total sales to identify business growth patterns.

- 🛒 **Top 10 Products**  
  Horizontal bar chart showing best-performing products by sales.

- 🧺 **Category & Sub-Category Breakdown**  
  Stacked bar chart comparing sales distribution within product categories and sub-categories.

- 🌍 **Sales by Region**  
  Bar chart displaying sales figures across different regions.

- 💼 **Summary Cards**  
  Simple card visuals showing Total Sales, Profit, and Orders (Using DAX measures).

---

## 🧰 Techniques Used

- 🔄 **SQL Aggregation**: Created separate summary tables for products, categories, regions, and monthly trends.
- 🎨 **Power BI Visuals**: Structured a clear, interactive layout with intuitive visuals and filtering capabilities.
